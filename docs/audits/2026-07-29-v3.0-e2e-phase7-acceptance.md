## Phase-7 findings (the re-run's own yield)

**E2E-P7-001 — HIGH — settings writes fail for every post-first account —
FIXED `30cf1ad` (+9 pins).** Surfaced by the sandbox re-run's
`risk preset: apply to the NON-active account` spec: `POST
/api/config/apply-preset` → 500 `settings write failed: 'No account_settings
row for account_id=2'`. Mechanism (investigated, two layers): (1)
`update_account_settings` was UPDATE-only — `add_account` never creates an
`account_settings` row and migration 001's seed is `LIMIT 1`, so every account
added after the initial migration 500s its first preset apply (the exact
config-2 "configure a second account before activating it" workflow) and
silently no-ops its settings saves (`routes_accounts`' settings writer is
except-pass); now the writer creates the row with the table's column defaults
iff the account exists. (2) Post-split `_resolve_db_path` scanned
`per_account/*.db` only — an account added after the split (legacy registry
row, no per-account file; nothing mints one until the deferred R1b lookup)
failed resolution before the writer even ran; now it falls back to the legacy
DB iff the account exists there, split-era accounts unchanged. Dormant on live
today (single account); loud the day a second account is added.
