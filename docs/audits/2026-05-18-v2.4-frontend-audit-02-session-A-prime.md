## Findings

### [FE-MED-018] [UX] Funding & Beta Exposure show "May 2026" header on LIVE-only views

  Description: Funding and Beta Exposure tabs are inherently "live snapshot" views — Funding shows current funding-rate exposure for open positions (with "Auto-refreshes every 30s" indicator and "FUNDING RATE EXPOSURE — LIVE" card title); Beta Exposure shows current beta-weighted exposure. Neither has a historical/period dimension. Yet the page-level period selector at top still shows `< May 2026 >` with Month / Week / Quarter / Year / 30D / 90D / All buttons. Buttons appear grayed/inactive on these tabs (good), but the date string remains, implying period scope the content doesn't have.

  Evidence: Screenshots ss_4163jzjjt (Funding "LIVE" card with period header still "May 2026"), ss_5684a1nt5 (Beta Exposure same pattern).

  Impact: User sees a "May 2026" framing for a card showing current state. The disabled buttons are a reasonable affordance but the title text is misleading.

  Recommended fix: When a sub-tab is fundamentally live/snapshot rather than period-scoped, hide the period header entirely (not just disable the buttons). Or replace "May 2026" with "Live" or current timestamp.

  Effort: Small (per-tab visibility flag for period header).

---

### [FE-MED-019] [Data] MFE/MAE detail table uses 4 decimal places on ENTRY column regardless of price scale

  Description: ENTRY column on MFE/MAE detail table shows uniform 4-decimal precision across all symbols:
  - `0.0749` (AIAUSDT) — appropriate for sub-cent assets.
  - `4.1005` (LABUSDT) — appropriate for small caps.
  - `676.7700` (BNBUSDT) — excessive (4 decimals on a $676 asset implies precision to ~$0.0001).
  - `2,340.3100` (ETHUSDT) — same issue.
  - `80,816.3000` (BTCUSDT) — same; 4 decimals on $80k is visually noisy and not market-meaningful.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_72621xzr6, ENTRY column rows AIAUSDT through BTCUSDT.

  Impact: Visually noisy on high-priced symbols. A trader scanning the column for outliers has to mentally trim trailing zeros.

  Recommended fix: Adaptive precision — for prices ≥ $1000, show 2 decimals; for $1–$1000, 4 decimals; for < $1, 4–6 decimals based on minimum tick. Or use exchange spec tick size. Same logic should apply across Calculator, History, etc. for consistency.

  Effort: Small — single formatting helper used across columns.

  Description: Calendar PnL has both:

  - **In-card month navigation**: separate `< Prev` / `Next >` buttons on either side of "May 2026" within the calendar card.

  Two independent month-navigation surfaces on the same view. Behavior of in-card Prev/Next while page-level shows "Quarter" or "Year" is unclear.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6 — page header at top; calendar card below with its own Prev/Next.

  Impact: Another instance of the period-selector duplication pattern already noted in audit-01 FE-MED-007 (Regime) and Session A FE-MED-017 (Equity Curve). Same root cause: missing PeriodSelector primitive.

  Recommended fix: Same as FE-MED-007 / FE-MED-017. Design the primitive to handle nested "current item" navigation as a built-in capability. Document precedence.

  Effort: Medium (primitive work resolves FE-MED-007 and FE-MED-017 too).

### [FE-LOW-010] [UI] Calendar PnL: no-data days and future days share the same visual treatment

  Description: On Calendar PnL for May 2026, three categories of empty cells exist:
  - **Layout-empty** (May 1–3 fall under Mon/Tue/Wed slots — prior-month-area).
  - **No-trade days within the period** (dark cell with `$0.00`).
  - **Future days** (May 20–31 — dark cell, no data).

  All three look essentially identical: dark background, no content.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6 — May 1–3 area, May 18–19, May 20–31.

  Impact: Cosmetic. Breaks cleanness of calendar at a glance.

  Recommended fix: Subtle visual distinction:
  - **Future days**: keep dark, slightly lighter; non-clickable.
  - **No-trade days within the period**: faint border or "no trades" subtitle.
  - **Out-of-month slots**: unrendered or lower opacity.

  Effort: Small.

### [FE-LOW-012] [UX] MFE/MAE summary labels lack methodology hints

  Description: MFE/MAE header summary row shows:
  - `AVG MFE $0.71`
  - `AVG |MAE| $1.56`
  - `AVG ME-RATIO 1.30`
  - `MFE >= 2× MAE 19.7%`

  None have a tooltip/help icon. Quant-literate trader will infer; newer user won't.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_72621xzr6.

  Impact: Cosmetic / minor. Same family as audit-01 MED-011/012.

  Recommended fix: Small `?` hover-tooltip beside each label explaining the calculation. `ME-RATIO`: "Average MFE divided by average |MAE|". `MFE >= 2× MAE`: "Percentage of trades where max favorable excursion was at least 2× max adverse excursion".

  Effort: Trivial.

### [FE-LOW-013] [Data] Calendar PnL day cell shows "$0.00" with "33%" win rate

  Description: May 17 (Sun) cell shows `$0.00` PnL with `33%` win rate, `3T` trades, `$81` volume. 33% win rate implies 1 winning trade of 3, but PnL nets to exactly `$0.00`. Mathematically possible (offsetting to 2-decimal precision after rounding) but suspiciously round. More likely: small float values rounded to 0.00 for display while the underlying total is something like $+0.003 or $-0.004.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6 — May 17 cell.

  Impact: Edge-case display precision. Misleading only if user reads "$0.00 with 33% win rate" as a paradox.

  Recommended fix: When PnL is within ±$0.005 of zero, display as `≈$0.00` or `<$0.01`. Or accept as expected behavior given small-account scale.

  Effort: Trivial.

## Cross-cutting observations

**Two well-designed empty states on Analytics.** R-Multiples ("No R-multiple data for this period. R-multiples come from the manual Trade History log (`individual_realized_r` field).") and Risk Metrics ("Insufficient data (need ≥ 20 trading days). Currently have 15 days.") are both clearly designed: they tell the user (a) what's missing, (b) why, (c) what action would resolve it. These should be reference templates for the EmptyState primitive. R-Multiples is slightly weaker — mentions a field name (`individual_realized_r`) non-developers won't recognize — minor improvement opportunity but the structure is right.

**The "live snapshot" vs "period-scoped" distinction isn't surfaced in the UI.** Five of 8 Analytics sub-tabs are period-scoped (Overview, Equity Curve, Calendar PnL, Traded Pairs, MFE/MAE, R-Multiples, Risk Metrics). Two are live-snapshot views (Funding, Beta Exposure). Current UX shoves the period header on both kinds identically, with only the buttons going gray on live sub-tabs. No clear signal that the framing differs.

**Period selector duplication now on four Analytics surfaces.** Audit-01 FE-MED-007 (Regime), Session A FE-MED-017 (Equity Curve), this session FE-MED-020 (Calendar PnL), and every other period-scoped sub-tab inheriting the page-level header. Most repeated pattern in the audit ledger. Build PeriodSelector primitive next — closes ~4 medium findings on its own.

**Analytics is structurally well-built.** With Session A 503-artifact out of the picture, all 7 sub-tabs render properly, show real data or designed empty states, respond fast. No console errors. Compared to other surfaces audited (Regime "As of undefined" and partially-backfilled cards from audit-01), Analytics is in better shape — remaining findings are polish-tier, not structural.

**Number formatting needs a centralized helper.** FE-MED-019 (4-decimal ENTRY regardless of price magnitude) is the most visible instance; audit-01 surfaces (Trade History decimals, Calculator preview precision) point to the same gap. A single `format_price(symbol, value)` helper consulting the exchange spec's tick size would close all in one move.
