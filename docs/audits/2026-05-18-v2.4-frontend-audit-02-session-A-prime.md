## Findings

### [FE-MED-018] [UX] Funding & Beta Exposure show "May 2026" header on LIVE-only views

  Description: Funding and Beta Exposure tabs are inherently "live snapshot" views — Funding shows current funding-rate exposure for open positions (with an "Auto-refreshes every 30s" indicator and "FUNDING RATE EXPOSURE — LIVE" card title); Beta Exposure shows current beta-weighted exposure. Neither has any historical/period dimension to it as currently designed. Yet the page-level period selector at top still shows `< May 2026 >` with Month / Week / Quarter / Year / 30D / 90D / All buttons. The buttons appear grayed/inactive on these tabs (good), but the date string remains, implying a period scope the content doesn't actually have.

  Evidence: Screenshots ss_4163jzjjt (Funding card titled "FUNDING RATE EXPOSURE — LIVE" with period header still showing "May 2026"), ss_5684a1nt5 (Beta Exposure showing same pattern).

  Impact: User sees a "May 2026" framing for a card that's actually showing current state. The disabled buttons are a reasonable affordance but the title text is misleading.

  Recommended fix: When a sub-tab is fundamentally live/snapshot rather than period-scoped, hide the period header entirely (not just disable the buttons). Or replace "May 2026" with "Live" or a current timestamp. Show date framing only on the sub-tabs where it actually applies (Overview, Equity Curve, Calendar PnL, Traded Pairs, MFE/MAE, R-Multiples, Risk Metrics).

  Effort: Small (per-tab visibility flag for period header).

### [FE-MED-019] [Data] MFE/MAE detail table uses 4 decimal places on ENTRY column regardless of price scale

  Description: ENTRY column on MFE/MAE detail table shows uniform 4-decimal precision across all symbols:
  - `0.0749` (AIAUSDT) — appropriate for sub-cent assets.
  - `4.1005` (LABUSDT) — appropriate for small caps.
  - `676.7700` (BNBUSDT) — excessive (4 decimals on a $676 asset implies precision to ~$0.0001; market doesn't trade that fine).
  - `2,340.3100` (ETHUSDT) — same issue.
  - `80,816.3000` (BTCUSDT) — same; 4 decimals on an $80k asset is visually noisy and not market-meaningful.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_72621xzr6, ENTRY column rows for AIAUSDT through BTCUSDT.

  Impact: Visually noisy on high-priced symbols. A trader scanning the column for outliers has to mentally trim trailing zeros. Slight cognitive load that accumulates across a 73-row table.

  Recommended fix: Adaptive precision — for prices ≥ $1000, show 2 decimals; for $1–$1000, show 4 decimals; for < $1, show 4–6 decimals based on minimum tick size. Or: use the symbol's actual tick-size precision from the exchange spec. Same logic should apply across all price displays (Calculator, History, etc.) to stay consistent.

  Effort: Small — likely a single formatting helper used across columns.

### [FE-MED-020] [Architecture] Calendar PnL adds yet another period-navigation surface — `< Prev` / `Next >` alongside the page-level period selector

  Description: Calendar PnL has both:
  - **Page-level period header**: `< May 2026 >` with Month / Week / Quarter / Year / 30D / 90D / All buttons.
  - **In-card month navigation**: separate `< Prev` and `Next >` buttons on either side of "May 2026" within the calendar card itself.

  Two independent month-navigation surfaces on the same view. Behavior of in-card Prev/Next while page-level shows "Quarter" or "Year" is unclear (does Prev/Next move month-by-month within the parent quarter? Or change the page-level period to Month?).

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6 — page header at top showing period selector; calendar card below with its own Prev/Next at row level.

  Impact: Another instance of the period-selector duplication pattern already noted in audit-01's FE-MED-007 (Regime) and Session A's FE-MED-017 (Equity Curve). Same root cause: missing PeriodSelector primitive that handles nested navigation cleanly.

  Recommended fix: Same as FE-MED-007 / FE-MED-017. As part of the PeriodSelector primitive work, design the primitive to handle a nested "current item" navigation (Prev/Next within the current period bucket) as a built-in capability. Document precedence: clicking Prev/Next moves the month pointer within the current period (so on "Quarter", Prev/Next moves between months within that quarter, hitting bounds when the quarter ends).

  Effort: Medium (primitive work that also resolves FE-MED-007 and FE-MED-017).

### [FE-LOW-010] [UI] Calendar PnL: no-data days and future days share the same visual treatment

  Description: On Calendar PnL for May 2026, three categories of empty cells exist:
  - **Layout-empty** (May 1–3 visually fall under Mon/Tue/Wed slots but they're prior-month-area, rendered dark).
  - **No-trade days within the period** (e.g., May 19 if no trades happened that day — dark cell with `$0.00`).
  - **Future days** (May 20–31 — dark cell, no data).

  Currently all three look essentially identical: dark background, no content. Semantically they're different states.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6 — May 1–3 area, May 18–19 (current day, partial data), May 20–31 (future).

  Impact: Cosmetic. Doesn't block use but breaks the cleanness of the calendar at a glance.

  Recommended fix: Subtle visual distinction:
  - **Future days**: keep dark, maybe slightly lighter than past/no-trade days; non-clickable.
  - **No-trade days within the period**: faint border or "no trades" subtitle.
  - **Out-of-month slots** (May 1–3 under Mon–Wed when May 1 is Fri): unrendered or visually de-emphasized (lower opacity).

  Effort: Small.

### [FE-LOW-011] [UI] Calendar PnL: cell layout uses unlabeled values in bottom corners

  Description: Each populated Calendar PnL day cell shows:
  - **Top-left**: day number (e.g., `4`)
  - **Top-right**: percentage (e.g., `44%`) — presumably win rate for the day
  - **Center-left**: PnL with sign (e.g., `-$1.00`, `+$0.42`)
  - **Bottom-left**: trade-count-like value (e.g., `1T`, `2T`, `5T`)
  - **Bottom-right**: dollar value (e.g., `$106`, `$208`, `$62`) — presumably volume

  No legend or hover-tooltip explains what bottom-left/bottom-right mean. The `T` suffix presumably means "trades" but isn't documented.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6.

  Impact: Same family as audit-01's FE-MED-011/012 — identifier-style/abbreviated labels with no explanation. Trader has to guess.

  Recommended fix: Hover-tooltip on cell explaining the four corners. Or a small legend below the calendar grid: `T = trades · $XXX = volume`. Or full labels if space permits.

  Effort: Trivial.

### [FE-LOW-012] [UX] MFE/MAE summary labels lack methodology hints

  Description: MFE/MAE page header summary row shows:
  - `AVG MFE $0.71`
  - `AVG |MAE| $1.56`
  - `AVG ME-RATIO 1.30`
  - `MFE >= 2× MAE 19.7%`

  None of these have a tooltip/help icon. A quant-literate trader will infer them; a newer user wouldn't.

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_72621xzr6 (summary row above the detail table).

  Impact: Cosmetic / minor. Same family as audit-01's MED-011/012 — methodology hints missing on dense quant metrics.

  Recommended fix: Small `?` hover-tooltip beside each label explaining the calculation. Specifically:
  - `ME-RATIO`: "Average MFE divided by average |MAE|"
  - `MFE >= 2× MAE`: "Percentage of trades where max favorable excursion was at least 2× max adverse excursion"

  Effort: Trivial.

### [FE-LOW-013] [Data] Calendar PnL day cell shows "$0.00" with "33%" win rate (May 17 Sun)

  Description: May 17 (Sun) cell shows `$0.00` PnL with `33%` win rate, `3T` trades, `$81` volume. The 33% win rate implies 1 winning trade out of 3, but the PnL nets to exactly `$0.00`. Mathematically possible (gains offset losses precisely to two-decimal precision after rounding) but suspiciously round.

  More likely: small float values rounded to 0.00 for display while the underlying total is something like $+0.003 or $-0.004. The display loses precision and shows what looks like "no PnL despite trades."

  Evidence: Screenshot ss_9104q6by6 — May 17 cell.

  Impact: Edge-case display precision issue. Misleading only if user reads "$0.00 with 33% win rate" as a paradox. Probably uncommon.

  Recommended fix: When PnL is within ±$0.005 of zero, display as `≈$0.00` or `<$0.01`. Or accept it as expected behavior given small-account scale.

  Effort: Trivial.

## Cross-cutting observations

**Two well-designed empty states on Analytics.** R-Multiples ("No R-multiple data for this period. R-multiples come from the manual Trade History log (`individual_realized_r` field).") and Risk Metrics ("Insufficient data (need ≥ 20 trading days). Currently have 15 days.") are both clearly designed: they tell the user (a) what's missing, (b) why it's missing, (c) what action would resolve it. These should be the reference templates when the EmptyState primitive is built. The R-Multiples version is slightly weaker because it mentions a field name (`individual_realized_r`) that non-developers won't recognize — minor improvement opportunity but the structure is right.

**The "live snapshot" vs "period-scoped" distinction isn't surfaced in the UI.** Five of the 8 Analytics sub-tabs (Overview, Equity Curve, Calendar PnL, Traded Pairs, MFE/MAE, R-Multiples, Risk Metrics) are period-scoped — they show data filtered by the page-level period selector. Two of the 8 (Funding, Beta Exposure) are live-snapshot views — current state regardless of selected period. The current UX shoves the period header on both kinds of views identically, with only the buttons going gray on live sub-tabs. The user has no clear signal that the framing is different.

**Period selector duplication has now surfaced on four Analytics surfaces.** Audit-01's FE-MED-007 was Regime. Session A's FE-MED-017 was Equity Curve. Session A''s FE-MED-020 is Calendar PnL. And every other period-scoped sub-tab inherits the page-level header. This is the most repeated pattern in the audit ledger now. Build the PeriodSelector primitive next — it would close ~4 medium findings on its own.

**Analytics is structurally well-built.** With the Session A 503-artifact removed from the picture, all 7 sub-tabs render properly, show real data or designed empty states, and respond fast. No console errors observed across the entire session. Compared to other surfaces audited (Regime Overview's "As of undefined" and partially-backfilled cards from audit-01), Analytics is in better shape — the remaining findings are polish-tier, not structural.

**Number formatting needs a centralized helper.** FE-MED-019 (4-decimal ENTRY regardless of price magnitude) is the most visible instance; audit-01 surfaces (Trade History decimals, Calculator preview precision) point to the same gap. A single `format_price(symbol, value)` helper that consults the exchange spec's tick size would close all these in one move.
