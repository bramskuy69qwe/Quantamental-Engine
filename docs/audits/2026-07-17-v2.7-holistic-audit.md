  out of a docs+surfaces commit; every one is hypothetical for the only

    classifies any signal CONTAINING the letters (e.g. `ATP`) as
    take_profit/stop_loss. exit_reason is display-tier; revisit with a
    real signal-name sample that misclassifies.
  - European-grouping deflation: `_to_float` strips `,` as a thousands
    separator, so a `1.234,56`-formatted cell would silently deflate.
    MC's observed exports carry numeric cells (no locale strings).
  - Time-only cells: `_to_iso` passes non-datetime, non-numeric cells
    through as strings — a time-only `Date` cell would yield a dateless
    dt string (sorts oddly, no crash).
  - Uncounted junk rows: rows neither `Entry*` nor `Exit*` are skipped
    without incrementing the `skipped` counter — the unpaired-rows log
    line under-reports pure-junk rows.
